Privacy Policy for Recallr

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: everything you save stays on your own device. The extension has no server, and nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Recallr is a browser extension that lets you save AI chat conversations and reusable prompts, and search them later.

What data the extension handles

When you click "Save chat", the extension copies the text of the conversation on the current page, its title and its web address. When you save a prompt, it stores the text you enter or select.

Where your data is stored

All saved chats and prompts are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. The extension has no server. Your data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. We do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not show ads.

Backups

If you use "Export backup", a file is saved to your computer, and you control that file. Importing a backup reads a file you choose, locally.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved chat or prompt inside the extension. Removing the extension from your browser deletes all of its stored data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
